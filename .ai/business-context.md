## What is MUSKOM?
MUSKOM is the official digital platform designed for community governance. It provides a secure, transparent, and seamless environment for managing the community musyawarah, participant registration, candidate profiling, attendance, voting, and real-time reporting.

## What is KOMITKABE?
KOMITKABE is the community body that utilizes MUSKOM for its governance and decision-making processes. The platform reflects their core values: sovereignty, transparency, and progressiveness.

## Who are the Users?
- **Public/Participants**: Community members who visit the landing page to view the musyawarah timeline, announcements, and candidate profiles. They also use the platform to register as participants and ultimately cast their votes.
- **Administrators/Panitia**: The official committee members who manage the musyawarah lifecycle, review registrations, manage content, and oversee the voting and attendance processes.

## Modules
- **Landing**: The public-facing portal showcasing the musyawarah status, timeline, candidates, and announcements.
- **Registration**: The entry point for participants and candidates to officially register.
- **Candidate**: The module for detailing and displaying candidate visions, missions, and profiles.
- **Attendance**: The check-in and presence tracking system for participants.
- **Voting**: The core secure module for casting and tabulating votes.
- **Result/Reporting**: The analytics and real-time calculation engine for election results.
- **Admin**: The operations center for the committee to manage all aspects of the system.

## Vision
To establish a premium, official, and trustworthy digital experience that elevates the standard of community governance and decision-making.

## Goals
- Provide a fast, reliable, and user-friendly portal.
- Ensure transparency and security in all transactional features (registration, voting).
- Empower the community with real-time, accurate reporting and seamless engagement.
