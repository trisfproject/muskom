# Product Vision

## Vision Statement
MUSKOM (Musyawarah Management System) is an enterprise-grade, AI-native digital platform designed to orchestrate complex institutional conventions, general assemblies, and elections (*Musyawarah*) with absolute transparency, security, and operational efficiency.

## Core Pillars
1. **End-to-End Governance:** Seamlessly connect event setup, delegate registration, administrative verification, candidate nominations, real-time attendance scanning, live quorum calculations, e-voting, and post-event resolution archiving into a single integrated platform.
2. **Architectural Integrity & Single Source of Truth:** Eliminate data fragmentation and domain duplication. Every business entity (Musyawarah, Participant, Candidate, Ballot, Attendance) has a single authoritative module owner.
3. **Auditability & Trust:** Every state mutation, verification approval, vote tally, and administrative override is recorded in an asynchronous, tamper-evident audit trail with full RBAC authorization.
4. **Resilient User Experience:** Mobile-first responsive front-end for delegates and public visitors, combined with an intuitive desktop workspace for committee members, verifiers, and administrators.
